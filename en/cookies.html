Cookie policy

Short and clear: this website uses as few cookies as possible, only what is needed to work properly.

Last updated: July 2026

What are cookies

Cookies are small text files that a website can place on your device. Among other things, they are used to make a site work properly and to remember preferences.

Which cookies we use

We use functional cookies only. These are necessary to make the website work properly and to remember that you have seen the cookie notice, so that it does not appear every time. No consent is required for these functional cookies.

What we do not use

We do not place tracking or advertising cookies and we do not follow your browsing behaviour across other websites. Your visit therefore stays as private as possible.

Managing cookies

You can always delete or block cookies yourself through your browser settings. Please note that the website may then not work properly in all respects.

Questions

If you have a question about this cookie policy, feel free to get in touch at contact@escort-international.nl. You can read more about how we handle data in our privacy policy.
