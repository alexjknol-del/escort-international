How it works

Finding a male escort does not have to be complicated. In a few calm steps you reach a choice that suits you.

1

Tell us what you are looking for

Let us know what you have in mind: the kind of company, the preferred place, a date and a time. Not sure yet? Then we think along with you. A first message is always without obligation.

2

Receive personal advice

Based on your wishes you receive a focused proposal, with honest information about who suits what you are looking for and what the cost is. No sales talk, but a sincere recommendation.

3

Make your choice

If it feels right, you confirm the appointment through the agency we refer to. You decide the location yourself, at your home or in a hotel. Until that moment you are committed to nothing.

4

Enjoy the meeting

The rest is up to you and your company. Afterwards it is always appreciated if you briefly let us know how it was. That may of course be entirely anonymous.

About the cost

Clear in advance, no surprises

Rates differ per male escort and are shared clearly with you beforehand. Travel costs are named separately, so that you never face unexpected amounts. For couples, extra surcharges are usually not charged.

Payment is usually partly in advance through the agency and partly directly to the male escort. So you always know in good time where you stand.

Good to know

  • A first request is always without obligation
  • Ask in good time, especially before the weekend
  • Getting in touch anonymously is possible
  • Your details stay with us and do not go to the male escort
