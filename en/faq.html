Frequently asked questions

The questions we hear most often, with an honest answer. If your question is not here, feel free to get in touch.

What about my privacy and discretion?

Discretion is our starting point. You do not need to create an account and your details are not shared with the gigolo or escort or with third parties. You decide how much you reveal, and you can get in touch anonymously, for instance from a withheld number.

Who are the introductions for?

For women and for couples (M-F). Many gigolos and escorts are also available for couples, and for couples extra surcharges are usually not charged. If you have a particular reason or some hesitation, we think along calmly.

What does it cost, and are there hidden charges?

Rates differ per person and are shared clearly with you in advance. We value sharp, affordable prices. Travel costs are named separately, so that you never face unexpected amounts. There are no hidden surcharges.

How does payment work?

Usually you pay part in advance through the agency and part directly to the gigolo or escort. The exact arrangements are gone through with you beforehand, so that you always know where you stand.

Do the gigolos and escorts also work abroad?

Yes. They travel throughout the Netherlands and Belgium, and abroad on request. Let us know where you would like the meeting, and we will look at what is possible and what any travel costs would be.

Can a meeting take place at my home or in a hotel?

Both are possible. You decide the location that feels pleasant and safe to you, whether that is at your home or in a hotel.

How far in advance should I request?

A short-notice request is sometimes possible, but the earlier you let us know, the greater the chance that your preference is available. Especially at the weekend it is wise to ask in good time.

How do I know it is reliable?

We deliberately refer to agencies that know their gigolos and escorts personally and select them in advance, rather than to advertising pages without oversight. That way what you see matches who you meet, and agreements are taken seriously.

Which agency do you refer to?

In practice we often refer to The Men's Company, a Dutch agency that has specialised since 2009 in gigolos for women and couples. If you would like to discuss things or if something else fits better, let us know.

How do I get in touch?

Simply by e-mail at contact@escort-international.nl. Tell us briefly what you are looking for and we will come back to you with honest, no-obligation advice.

Is your question not listed?

Feel free to ask it. You will get an honest answer, with no obligations.
