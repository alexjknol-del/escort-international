About Escort International

A calm, honest starting point for anyone looking for a reliable male escort, at home or across the border.

Escort International grew out of a simple observation: anyone looking for company quickly gets lost among advertising pages where no one is really responsible for what is promised. Photos that do not match, agreements that fade away, and the question of whether someone can be trusted. That can be done better, and above all more calmly.

So we deliberately take a different route. Escort International is an introducer, not an advertising marketplace. We refer to male escorts through agencies that know their people personally and select them carefully in advance. That takes the gamble out of the process and leaves you with a pleasant, relaxed meeting.

What we believe in

Our core values

Discretion

Your privacy is the starting point, not an afterthought. No account is needed, details are not shared with the male escort, and you decide how much you reveal.

Reliability

We refer only to parties who take their work seriously. Selection, agreements and aftercare are all part of that. You know beforehand where you stand.

Fair rates

We believe company should stay within reach. That is why we emphasise clear, sharp prices without hidden surcharges or unexpected costs afterwards.

Personal attention

Behind every request is a person with their own wishes and sometimes a little hesitation. We take the time to listen and give honest advice, even if that means advising against something.

For whom

Women and couples

Our introductions are meant for women and for couples (M-F). For couples who want to experience something together, such as the company of a male escort, there is plenty of experience and extra surcharges are usually not charged. Anyone with a particular reason, for instance after a less pleasant experience in the past, is also welcome to discuss things without obligation. Extra care is taken in such cases.

It is not about spending as much as possible. It is about getting it right: the right person, a pleasant atmosphere and the feeling that you can relax.

Our recommendation

An agency we trust

That does not mean it is the only choice for everyone. Tell us what you are looking for and we will think along about what fits best.
