Privacy policy

Your privacy is the starting point of how we work. Below you can read how we handle your data.

Last updated: July 2026

Who we are

Escort International is an introduction and information site that helps women and couples find carefully selected male escorts. For questions about this privacy policy you can reach us at contact@escort-international.nl.

Which data we process

We process only the data you provide yourself when you get in touch, for example by e-mail. This usually concerns your e-mail address, possibly a name or form of address and the content of your message, such as your wishes and the preferred place and date. You are never obliged to share more than you want, and getting in touch anonymously is possible.

What we use your data for

We use your data only to answer your question, to give you suitable advice and, if you wish, to put you in contact with a suitable agency or companion. We do not use your data for advertising and we do not sell it.

Sharing with third parties

Your data is not shared with third parties as a matter of course. Only when you yourself choose to make an appointment do we share, with your consent, the strictly necessary information with the agency concerned. Your data is never provided directly to the male escort.

Retention period

We do not keep your data longer than necessary for the purpose for which you provided it. Correspondence that does not lead to an appointment is deleted after it has been dealt with.

Security

We take appropriate measures to protect your data against loss or unauthorised access. Our communication channels are secured and we treat everything you entrust to us confidentially.

Cookies

This website uses only functional cookies that are needed to make the site work properly. You can read more about this in our cookie policy.

Your rights

You have the right to view, correct or delete the data we process about you. You can send such a request to contact@escort-international.nl. We respond as quickly as possible. If you believe we are not handling your data carefully, you can file a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority.

Changes

We may adjust this privacy policy from time to time. The most current version is always available on this page.
